Learning TA support

Learn chart reading, indicators, financial statements and risk through lessons, native exercises and explained practice questions.

Free lessons and permanent access

Lessons 1–5 and their exercises are free. A single non-consumable purchase permanently unlocks lessons 6–53 and their labs and quizzes. There is no subscription. The US base price is US$20; Apple shows your storefront’s localized price before you confirm.

Restore a purchase

Open You → Restore Purchases, or select Restore Purchases on the unlock screen. Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account that bought the course. Apple manages payments and refunds. We never need your Apple Account password.

Offline study and progress

Lessons and exercises are bundled. After access is verified, you can study offline. A current price lookup, purchase or restore needs an Apple connection.

Learning position and quiz history are saved on each device. This version does not synchronize learning progress. Resetting learning progress does not remove your Apple purchase. Restoring a purchase does not restore deleted lesson history.

Report a problem

Use the Crow Tech contact form. Include the app version, lesson title, device and iOS version, what you did, what happened and what you expected. A screenshot can help. During beta testing, you can also send feedback through TestFlight.

Do not include payment-card information, financial account credentials or private keys.

About the exercises

Exercises use clearly labelled synthetic data and simplified teaching models. The app does not provide live market feeds, personalized investment recommendations or promises of trading results.
