Learning TA privacy policy

Learning Technical Analysis for iPhone and iPad · Updated 19 October 2026

Learning TA is an educational app published by Crow Tech Pte. Ltd. This policy describes the app’s local learning records, purchases and optional support.

Learning records stay on your device

The app stores lesson position, completed lessons, quiz attempts and scores on your device. This version has no learning account, developer-operated learning server or progress synchronization. Crow Tech does not receive these records.

Use You → Reset learning progress to remove the app’s local learning records. Deleting the app can also remove them. Device backups are managed by your Apple device settings.

Apple manages purchases

Apple handles the permanent full-course purchase and payment. The app uses verified StoreKit transactions to determine access. Crow Tech does not receive payment-card details. Your purchase is associated with your Apple Account and can be restored independently of local learning progress.

Looking up prices, buying or restoring can communicate with Apple and require an internet connection. Lessons and synthetic exercises are bundled in the app.

No developer tracking or analytics

This version contains no advertising, tracking code, third-party analytics SDK, developer account service or live market-data service. The app does not request your contacts, precise location or financial account credentials.

Support and beta testing

If you choose to contact us, Crow Tech receives the information you include and uses it to respond to your request. Use the Crow Tech contact form for support or privacy requests. Do not send payment-card details, financial account credentials or private keys.

During a TestFlight beta, Apple provides diagnostics and tester feedback through TestFlight under its own privacy information and terms. These services are separate from the app’s local learning records.

External websites

Source links and support pages open external services governed by their own policies. Visiting a website can expose ordinary connection information to the website and its hosting provider. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; it contains no developer analytics scripts or advertising.

Contact and changes

Contact Crow Tech Pte. Ltd. about privacy, including optional support correspondence. If a future version adds accounts, synchronization or data collection, we will update this policy and the app’s privacy information.
